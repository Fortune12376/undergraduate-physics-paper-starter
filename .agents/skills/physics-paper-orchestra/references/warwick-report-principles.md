# Retained Physics Report Writing principles (reconstructed summary)
The current `@write-physics-reports` was created November 2026 using lab-feedback synthesis, marking criteria, a PX428 transcript, and an older PRL writing skill. Its full content was not accessible for export. The following are retained from the known decisions, not claimed as verbatim content.

- Separate Warwick coursework report and genuine PRL/PNAS submission modes.
- Check whether critical physical theory is missing, present but difficult to locate, or unanswerable given the evidence.
- Prefer mark-critical scientific improvements over generic polishing.
- Map the actual assessment criteria onto specific locations and evidence in the report; do not assume feedback is consistent with the awarded rubric categories.
- Explain uncertainty method (blocking, bootstrap, autocorrelation, detrending) when its use affects interpretation, rather than adding unrelated textbook background.
- Preserve nuanced outcomes such as unresolved simulation trials and operational classification thresholds.
- Scientific review and prose editing are different tasks. Raise substantive issues first.
