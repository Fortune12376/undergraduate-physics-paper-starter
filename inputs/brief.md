# Project brief

Fill in what you know and link to files under `inputs/`.

- Working title and research question
- Module or journal, audience and assignment brief
- Required template, sections and marking rubric
- Paths to past marker or supervisor feedback, with recurring issues you want to address
- Applicable style guidance (for example, APS conventions or a journal's instructions)
- Permitted AI assistance and required disclosures, if applicable
- Theory or prediction to test
- What you actually did, including any departures from the original plan
- Main results, units, uncertainties and their source files
- Figures, analysis code and references to use
- Open questions or decisions to raise at plan review

## Length and page budget

- Hard limit and unit (pages or words) and where the rule is stated
- What counts toward the limit (title, abstract, figures, tables, references and appendices)
- Target for counted material. Default to using as much of the allowance as the evidence and rubric support. Enter a different target if you prefer a shorter paper.
- Sections to prioritise if there is room (for example, uncertainty analysis, comparison with theory or limitations supported by the data)

## Optional Google Deep Review

- Send the complete near-final PDF to Google Deep Review at checkpoint 3 for one free review. **Yes** (default). Set to **No** to skip external review for this paper.
- Assignment or publication rule that permits external AI review, if applicable

Codex includes this setting in the plan you approve. If the applicable rules permit external AI review, Codex checks free quota after the internal audit and PDF inspection.
